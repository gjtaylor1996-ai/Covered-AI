FAQ

Common questions

Is this just another staffing agency? +

No — Covered doesn't employ workers or take on the statutory costs of being their employer. It's a matching and verification layer, which is why the commission sits well below a typical agency markup.

Do workers pay anything to use Covered? +

No, and that's deliberate — charging workers would slow the exact supply growth that makes the platform valuable to venues.

How is the Reliability Score different from a star rating? +

It's built from behavioural data — attendance, punctuality, simple yes/no venue confirmations — not a 1-5 average that's easy to inflate or game with social pressure.

How fast can I book someone? +

Search, filter and shortlist takes minutes. Actual response time depends on the worker's availability window and how far in advance you post the shift — the "Need someone tonight" quick action is built for last-minute cover specifically.

Is Covered live yet? +

Covered is currently in prototype and pre-pilot stage — the product you can click through is fully built, but there's no live production version with real venues yet. Join the list below to hear when pilot spots open up.
